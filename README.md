# Chapter 10: Open When… ♡
> *“little letters for all your moods ❤️”*

A romantic, intimate interactive digital scrapbook made for Chapter 10 of a love story.

---

### ✨ Features
- **Warm & Cozy Aesthetic**: Cream paper texture, soft pastel tones, and handwritten typography.
- **Six Open-When Letters**:
  - 💗 Open When You Miss Me
  - 💙 Open When You're Sad
  - 🧡 Open When You're Mad at Me
  - 💜 Open When You Can't Sleep
  - 💚 Open When You Need a Hug
  - 🎂 Open on Your Birthday
- **Interactive Envelopes**: Gentle flap-opening animations transitioning into personalized manga letter pages.
- **Custom Voice Note Player**: Handcrafted music card player for personal audio recordings.
- **100% Client-Side**: Pure HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript. Ready for GitHub Pages or Vercel.
